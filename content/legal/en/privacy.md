## What we store

- Account data: your email address, a password hash or passkey, and the sessions that keep you signed in.
- Tasks and planning data: tasks, projects, goals, habits, weekly resets and focus sessions that you create.
- Session data: the time of sign-in, IP address and browser identification, so that you stay signed in and can review your active sessions.
- Product events without content: short counters such as a ritual opened or an anchor done. They never carry the text you typed and no IP address.
- Push subscriptions: the endpoint and keys your browser issues when you turn reminders on.

## Why we process it

- Account and planning data: to perform our contract with you (Art. 6 (1) b GDPR).
- Secure operation and abuse prevention: our legitimate interest in a working, safe service (Art. 6 (1) f GDPR).
- Reminders and push messages: your consent (Art. 6 (1) a GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time in the app.
- Invoices and tax records: a legal obligation (Art. 6 (1) c GDPR).

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising.

## Cookies and tracking

We use one technically necessary session cookie to keep you signed in. There is no tracking, no advertising and no analytics from third parties. Paddle.js runs only on the checkout page.

## How long we keep data

- Your account and planning data stay while your account exists.
- An account without access and without sign-in for 12 months is deleted. We warn you 30 days and 7 days before.
- When you delete your account in the app, we remove the data within 30 days.

## Your rights

You can access, correct, export and delete your data. Export and account deletion live in the app under Settings. You also have the right to restrict processing, to object and to data portability. Write to [[CONTACT_EMAIL]] for any request. You can complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular the one where you live or where the controller is based.
