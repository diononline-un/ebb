# Terms

## The service

Ebbe is a planning app for tasks, projects, goals and habits. It runs as a web app and as a progressive web app. The contract is between you and [[OPERATOR_NAME]].

## Trial

New accounts start with a free trial. When the trial ends, everything you created stays available: you can still read and export it. Writing new plans needs an active subscription.

## Subscription and payment

Subscriptions run through Paddle, which acts as Merchant of Record. Paddle takes the payment, sends invoices and handles applicable taxes. You manage payment details and invoices in the Paddle customer portal, which the app links to.

## Cancellation

You can cancel at any time in the Paddle customer portal. The subscription ends at the end of the current billing period; access stays until then.

## Availability

We work to keep the service available, but we do not guarantee uninterrupted availability. Maintenance, updates and outages can interrupt the service. Backups run daily so that your data survives a failure.
